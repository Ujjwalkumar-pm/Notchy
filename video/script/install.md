## Verification notes

### Gatekeeper wording (I3): macOS 15 dialog drawn, voice and caption version-neutral
- **Why macOS 15:** it is the flow most new users will see on macOS 15 and later. **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security works on both macOS 14 and 15. The old Control-click → Open shortcut no longer bypasses the block on macOS 15, so the video doesn't teach it.
- **Dialog drawn (macOS 15.x, US English):** title **""Notchy" Not Opened"**, body **"Apple could not verify "Notchy" is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy."**, buttons **Done** / **Move to Trash**.
- **macOS 14 wording differs:**
  - "…can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software" is the Developer-ID, not-notarized case.
  - Notchy is self-signed ("Notchy Local Signing", no Team ID), so macOS 14 may instead show "…can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer."
  - The way through is the same: **Open Anyway**.
- **Caption and voice stay generic** ("macOS can't verify Notchy"), so they are true on 14 and 15.
- **Before the final render:** run one real check on a clean user account. Download the release zip in Safari (that sets quarantine), open the app, and confirm:
  - the dialog title,
  - the button names,
  - the **Open Anyway** line in Privacy & Security,
  - the second confirm step (password or Touch ID).
- **Open Anyway might not appear:** some web reports say the button is missing for some unsigned apps on 15.x. Notchy is signed with hardened runtime, so it should appear.

### Other install facts checked in code
- **Start at login is turned on by itself at first launch** (Lifecycle.swift:38-50, AppDelegate.swift:63), so macOS shows a "login item added" style notification. This is now covered by the I6 caption.
- **First launch no longer quits any other app** (removed 16 Nov at the owner's request). Not shown in the video.
- **Without a notch:** the panel uses a 200×32 top-center fallback (NotchGeometry.swift:25, 40-51), so "best on MacBooks with a notch" is accurate.
- **Unread counts** come from Dock badges, so Teams and Outlook have to be running and in the Dock (DockBadges.swift:5-8). Not voiced.
