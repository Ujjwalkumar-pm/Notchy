## Copy rules applied
- No hype words and no other notch-app names. Third-party names appear in voice and captions only (Teams, Outlook, AirPods).
- Neutral wording (owner request, 16 Nov): voice, captions, covers, post copy and README never name the coding agent or its maker. It is "your AI coding agent" / "agent approvals". No claim that other agents work. Small UI labels inside the real footage are left as captured, and no caption is placed on them. No third-party logos; app tiles use the in-app initials (AlertsTab.swift:205).
- No claims of Apple approval, notarization or the App Store.
- "Allow once" is the real button label, not "Allow" (ApprovalCard.swift:106).
- US spelling throughout: center, gray, license, Trash.
